chasing a pattern, notes from an untrained mind
What this is. The record of two minds chasing a pattern, told in order, including the parts that were wrong. Amateur research, done for the love of numbers. No formal training in mathematics here, just a thinking partner in AI and some ideas that go well outside the norm. Nothing is claimed as proven. A human and a machine, working out loud, through one of the most notorious open problems in mathematics.
